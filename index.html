ZABATA is built so drivers and riders keep most of what they earn, and customers pay fair, clear prices.

💰 How drivers & riders earn more

  • You keep 90%. ZABATA takes only 10% of your weekly profit.
  • The customer pays you directly by cash or bank transfer.
  • Go online at busy times (7–10am and 5–9pm).
  • Accept more, cancel less.
Example: you earn ₦50,000 in one week.
ZABATA 10% = ₦5,000
You keep = ₦45,000

🏷️ How customers save more

  • Clear prices. You see the price before you send your request.
  • Earn Coins. Every finished trip gives you 1 Coin. 8 Coins = free trip.

📋 What it costs to join

  • ₦1,000 every month subscription.
  • 10% every week of your earnings.

About ZABATA

ZABATA

LAGOS DELIVERY & RIDE

ZABATA is Nigeria’s modern ride-hailing and delivery platform built for comfort, speed and fair prices. Whether you need a car, a motorbike or a quick package delivery, ZABATA connects you with verified drivers and riders across the country.

🤑 Become a partner with ZABATA

Keep 90% of your earnings with a simple ₦1,000 monthly subscription. Drive a car or ride a bike, get paid directly by customers, and complete trips to earn partner coins: reach the coin goal and your next week is 100% profit before ZABATA’s 10% resumes.
